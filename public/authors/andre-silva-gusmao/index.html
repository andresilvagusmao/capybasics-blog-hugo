About ME
#

I’m Brazilian, married, and the proud father of two plants, John and Daniel.

My first experience in software development was a part-time role back in 2016. Since then, every position I’ve held — whether full-time or part-time — has been in the tech field.

I hold a Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science from PUC Minas, Brazil, and a Master’s degree in Applied Computer Science from the Norwegian University of Science and Technology (NTNU) in Norway.

Outside of programming, I enjoy comedy shows like The Office and Curb Your Enthusiasm. I’m also a fan of classic horror B-movies such as The Evil Dead and Killer Klowns from Outer Space.
