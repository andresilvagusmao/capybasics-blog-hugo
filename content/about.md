I’m André Silva Gusmão, a software engineer from Belo Horizonte, Brazil. I have a master’s degree in Applied Computer Science from the Norwegian University of Science and Technology (NTNU), and experience building software across consulting, cloud engineering, global trade, and applied AI.

My work has ranged from backend services and cloud infrastructure to web applications and research prototypes. I’ve worked with teams and clients at Thoughtworks, Ben Fatto, Base2, Bosch, NTNU, Sykehuset Innlandet, SINTEF, Ref Spatial, and Engesoft.

## Capybasics

Capybasics is my personal project for sharing approachable introductions to software engineering and web development. The name reflects its focus on the basics: I write about and make videos on topics I’m learning or find interesting, even when those topics aren’t closely related.

Visit the [Capybasics YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@Capybasics) or browse the [projects page](/projects/) to learn more.
