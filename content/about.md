Welcome to Capybasics. 

Capybasics is a side project created by André Silva Gusmão that aims to teach software engineering and web development for everybody.

The idea is to cover the Basics, hence the name capyBASICS, of different topics related to software development.
I will try to make blog posts and videos about the basics of the topics I aim to teach.

You might notice that the topics could have little to no link between them. This happens because since this is a side project I want to cover only topics that I find interesting at the moment I am studying them.

I also post videos about the topics in my youtube channel: 

https://www.youtube.com/@Capybasics
