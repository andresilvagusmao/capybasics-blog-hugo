# Selected Projects

Here are a few projects that represent my research, professional contributions, and personal interests. Some work was private, discontinued, or covered by client agreements, so I have not listed those projects individually.

## SemML

**Research project and publication**

[SemML: Facilitating development of ML models for condition monitoring with semantics](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1570826821000391?via%3Dihub) is a research project and paper published in the *Journal of Web Semantics*. The work explored a condition-monitoring system for industrial processes, combining machine learning with semantic technologies.

I contributed to the research and to the development and prototyping of the system’s user interface, primarily using Angular and TypeScript.

## SIndAIS4

**Research collaboration**

[SIndAIS4: Scaling Industrial AI with Semantics](https://sirius-labs.no/sindais4-scaling-industrial-ai-with-semantics/) is an initiative from the SIRIUS Centre for Research-based Innovation. I contributed to work connected to SemML, including documenting and developing the user interface for a Bosch automated-welding quality-monitoring system.

## Capybasics

**Personal project**

[Capybasics](https://www.youtube.com/@Capybasics) is my project for making software engineering and web development topics approachable through blog posts and videos. I choose topics based on what I’m learning and interested in, and explain them starting with the fundamentals.

## Other professional work

I have also contributed to commercial applications and prototypes in consulting, civil engineering, healthcare, and industrial research. This work has included backend services, web applications, authentication integrations, and cloud infrastructure. I don’t list client-specific details here where the work is private or subject to confidentiality.
